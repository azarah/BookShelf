About Us

The steward of the shelf

Open Garden Archipelago is a bookstore of linked realms — stories that share roots beneath different skies.

Who we are

This site gathers chronicles by a network of writers into one browsing place: gardens between domains, dawns before repentance, salt craft between stars, neon codes under corporate grids, and diaries that ask who defines reality.

Built as a CIS 181 final project, the archipelago is meant to feel like a bookstore front, however a store that open each story’s own world of color, type, and layout.

What we believe

  • Stories can share roots without sharing the same skin.
  • Readers should choose a cover, not a spreadsheet of links.
  • Quiet systems — gardens, gradients, covenants — still shape the feed.

Studio side project

Open Garden Archipelago is a side project of Charletta Hudson, a writer and developer. It is a way to explore the intersection of writing and technology.
